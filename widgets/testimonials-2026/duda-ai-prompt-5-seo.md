Add a tab called "SEO" to the Content Editor of this widget. Add these fields to that tab only. Keep the keys exactly as written.

**Tab: SEO**

- Add review markup for search engines | key: schemaEnabled | type: toggle | default: false | help: Adds hidden review and star-rating data. Only testimonials with a real star rating are included, and nothing is added when none have one. Search engines decide whether to show stars, and Google does not usually show stars for a business's reviews of itself.
- Business name | key: schemaBusinessName | type: text | default: (empty) | help: Required for the markup. The name of the business the reviews are about.
- Business type | key: schemaBusinessType | type: dropdown | default: Organization | options: Organization, LocalBusiness, EmploymentAgency | help: The kind of business being reviewed.
