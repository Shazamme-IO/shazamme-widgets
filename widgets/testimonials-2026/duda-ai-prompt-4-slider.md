Add a tab called "Slider" to the Content Editor of this widget. Add these fields to that tab only. Keep the keys exactly as written. They apply to the slider and featured layouts.

**Tab: Slider**

- Previous and next buttons | key: arrows | type: toggle | default: true | help: Visitors can also swipe on a touch screen.
- Pagination | key: pagination | type: dropdown | default: dots | options: dots, avatars, none | help: Dots are small buttons. Avatars are the people's photos or initials.
- Loop | key: loop | type: toggle | default: true | help: On: after the last testimonial it goes back to the first.
- Autoplay | key: autoplay | type: toggle | default: false | help: Moves on by itself. It pauses when the mouse is over it or a button has focus, and a pause button appears. It never moves for visitors who ask their device to reduce motion.
- Seconds on each testimonial | key: autoplaySeconds | type: number | default: 6 | help: 2 to 60.
- Name for screen readers | key: ariaLabel | type: text | default: Client testimonials | help: Read out by screen readers. It is not shown on the page.
