Build the Design Editor for this widget. Every line is one style a site builder can change. Keep the selectors and properties exactly as written. Group under the headings.

**Heading**

- Line height | selector: .shm-ty .shm-ty-heading | property: line-height | default: 1.1
- Alignment (left, center, right) | selector: .shm-ty .shm-ty-heading | property: text-align | default: left
- Font | selector: .shm-ty .shm-ty-heading | property: font-family | default: inherit

**Lead text**

- Lead colour | selector: .shm-ty .shm-ty-lead | property: color | default: #00afce
- Lead weight | selector: .shm-ty .shm-ty-lead | property: font-weight | default: 200

**Cycling words**

- Word colour | selector: .shm-ty .shm-ty-word | property: color | default: #101920
- Word weight | selector: .shm-ty .shm-ty-word | property: font-weight | default: 700

**Outlined words (used when "Outlined words" is on)**

- Outline colour | selector: .shm-ty.is-outlined .shm-ty-word | property: -webkit-text-stroke-color | default: #101920
- Outline width | selector: .shm-ty.is-outlined .shm-ty-word | property: -webkit-text-stroke-width | default: 2px
- Fill inside the outline | selector: .shm-ty.is-outlined .shm-ty-word | property: -webkit-text-fill-color | default: transparent

**Pause button (shows only when focused with the keyboard)**

- Pause button text colour | selector: .shm-ty .shm-ty-pause:focus-visible | property: color | default: #ffffff
- Pause button background | selector: .shm-ty .shm-ty-pause:focus-visible | property: background-color | default: #101920
