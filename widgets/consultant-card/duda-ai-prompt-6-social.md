Add a tab called "Social" to the Content Editor of this widget. Add these fields to that tab only. Keep the keys exactly as written. Each network shows only for consultants who have that link in the Shazamme console, and opens in a new tab.

**Tab: Social**

LinkedIn
- Show LinkedIn | key: showLinkedin | type: toggle | default: false | help: A link to the consultant's LinkedIn profile.
- LinkedIn icon | key: showLinkedinIcon | type: toggle | default: true | help: The LinkedIn logo.
- LinkedIn text | key: showLinkedinLabel | type: toggle | default: false | help: Shows the words below beside the logo.
- LinkedIn words | key: linkedinText | type: text | default: LinkedIn | help: Also read out by screen readers.
