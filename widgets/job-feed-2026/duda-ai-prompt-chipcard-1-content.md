Add a new section to the Content Editor of this widget, below all the existing sections. Do NOT change, move, rename or reset any existing field. Name the section "Card style". Every field below is new.

**Card style**

- Card style | key: cardStyle | type: dropdown | default: standard | options: standard, chips | help: "standard" keeps the card this feed has always had. "chips" shows a compact card: title, location, work type and work model chips, and the pay line, with the whole card linking to the job.
- Show location on chip card | key: chipShowLocation | type: toggle | default: true | help: City and state under the title. Chip card only.
- Show country on chip card | key: chipShowCountry | type: toggle | default: false | help: Adds the country after the state. Chip card only.
- Show work type chip | key: chipShowWorkType | type: toggle | default: true | help: For example Permanent or Contract. Chip card only.
- Show work model chip | key: chipShowWorkModel | type: toggle | default: true | help: For example Hybrid or Onsite. Chip card only.
- Show pay line | key: chipShowSalary | type: toggle | default: true | help: Shows the job's pay text, or the from and to amounts when there is no pay text. Chip card only.
